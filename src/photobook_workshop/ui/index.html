First time here? Start with these three steps.
  1. Explore. Use the page buttons to look through your book. The included sample is entirely invented.
  2. Choose. Click a caption, or leave it unchosen. Notes are instructions for later edits, not text added to the book.
  3. Make. “Make a proof” builds a PDF, browser pages and paper checklists in your local review folder. Your source photos stay intact.

Nothing on this screen uploads photos or uses an AI service. For your own book, follow the included Getting Started guide or use the assistant skill.
